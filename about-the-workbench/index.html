ABOUT THE WORKBENCH

A home for small, useful ideas.

There is always one cable, one tool, one awkward gap that could use a better solution.

Goblin Workbench is a maker brand for original parametric designs: practical objects built from simple geometry, with room for you to change the dimensions. We like tidy cable runs, tools within reach and source files that explain themselves.

A useful kind of mischief.

The goblin stands for noticing the overlooked job and trying a small fix. The workbench stands for the process: measure, make a test piece, learn what needs changing, and try again.

Open the lid.

Each design family includes STL exports, editable OpenSCAD source, print starting points and a dimension guide. Geometry, renders and listing copy are CC-BY-4.0; code is MIT. AI-assisted CAD scripting was used, and no third-party model was imported.

Evidence before promises.

Digital checks help us inspect geometry. They cannot tell us how a print will fit, hold a load or age on your bench. Physical trials are still pending, and renders are always labeled. We would rather give you a clear starting point than an unmeasured guarantee.
