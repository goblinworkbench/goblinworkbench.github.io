FROM FILE TO FIT

A little measuring
goes a long way.

01 — MEASURE

Start with the real thing.

Measure the cable, key or spool core you actually own. Every family ships a small test coupon so the first print is quick and cheap.

02 — ADJUST

Turn the dials.

Open the OpenSCAD source and change a dimension. The parametric model rebuilds around your numbers.

03 — PRINT

Try it on the bench.

Export your STL, print the coupon, check the fit, tune and repeat before printing the full part.

04 — TIDY

Enjoy the small fix.

Keys in reach, cables in line, desiccant in the spool core. Small, useful, and yours to remix.
